Shadow Stealth – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Shadow Stealth ("the App") handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, request, transmit or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

2. Local Data Storage

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times) and settings are stored only on your device in a local database. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the App.

3. Internet, Ads and Third Parties

The App works fully offline. It does not use any online API, analytics, advertising network or third-party tracking service.

4. Permissions

The App does not require any sensitive permissions. Vibration feedback, if enabled in settings, uses the standard device haptic feature.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the App collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Your Consent

By using Shadow Stealth, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
